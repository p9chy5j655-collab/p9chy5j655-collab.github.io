      Frequently asked

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. The page loads once and every tool then runs in your browser. Your PDFs and photos are opened in memory, processed there, and saved straight back to your device.

      Is it really free? What is the catch?

      It is free and there is no sign-up. Because there is no server doing the work, there is no cost to pass on to you. Future ads on the page keep it that way.

      Which formats can I use?

      PDF for merging and splitting; JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and BMP for the image tools. PDFs protected by a password are not supported.

      Does the mosaic really remove the information?

      The blurred area is redrawn as coarse blocks and the result is exported as a new PNG, so the original pixels under that area do not survive in the file you download.
